Privacy policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Magic Time doesn’t collect any data. It has no accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking, and it never connects to the internet. Everything it does happens on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your time zone choice. Magic Time remembers the time zone you pick so it’s there next time. On Mac, it also remembers which format you last highlighted. These are stored in the app’s own settings on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • Open at Login, on Mac. If you turn this on, macOS keeps the setting. You can turn it off in Magic Time or in System Settings.

What you type isn’t saved. Magic Time reads it to work out the time, then lets it go.

Clipboard

When you copy a format, Magic Time puts that code on your clipboard. It never reads what’s already on your clipboard.

Siri, Shortcuts, and Spotlight

When you use Make a Discord Timestamp from Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, or the Action button, the phrase you give it is handled by Magic Time on your device, the same way as in the app. Siri’s own handling of your voice is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

Magic Time doesn’t collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Questions

Ask on GitHub. Magic Time is open source, so you can also read the code to see exactly what it does.
